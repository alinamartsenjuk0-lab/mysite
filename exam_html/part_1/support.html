Frequently Asked Questions & Knowledge Base

How do I reset my account password?

To reset your password, go to the "User Profile" section and click "Change Password" or use the recovery link on the login page.

Why are my sales metrics not syncing?

Data synchronizes every 15 minutes. If you notice a longer delay, please check your API connection status in the settings.

Where can I download monthly financial reports?

All reports are available on the Dashboard page via the export button in the upper right corner of the table.

How do I add a new team member?

Navigate to Account Settings, open the "Team" tab, and send an invitation to the new employee's email address.

How do I set up two-factor authentication?

In your user profile, enable the 2FA toggle switch and scan the QR code using the Google Authenticator app.

Trouble logging in with Google?

Clear your browser cache or ensure that your Google account email matches the one used during registration.

How do I change my company name?

Go to the "User Profile" page, update the value in the "Company" field, and click the "Update Profile" button.

CRM System Integration

Our platform supports integration via Webhooks and REST API. Detailed documentation can be generated in settings.

How do I generate a report for a specific period?

Use the date range filter on the Dashboard page to select your desired timeframe of days or months.

Managing notification settings

In the Notifications section, you can configure the specific types of system and financial alerts you wish to receive.
